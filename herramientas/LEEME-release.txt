Nerd Pocket: a virtual analog synth with a step sequencer and an arpeggiator, for the M-VAVE FM-1.
Unofficial firmware. Not affiliated with or endorsed by M-VAVE. Use it at your own risk.

Source of the FM-1 app (MPL-2.0): https://github.com/jconde007/nerd-pocket
The synth engine is closed source: (c) 2026 Jorge Conde, free to use with this firmware.
Web installer and browser emulator: https://jconde007.github.io/nerd-pocket/
Licence of the app: MPL-2.0 (LICENSE-MPL-2.0.txt). It includes three files of JieLi's AC79 SDK (Apache-2.0) and the Barlow font (SIL OFL 1.1), in LICENSES/.
Built on fm1-base and felucca-bsp (MPL-2.0).

BEFORE INSTALLING
- It uses the FM-1's second core, which is beta and misbehaves on some units. If it fails, the synth keeps running on one core with fewer voices until you switch it off.
- It has been tested on a single FM-1.
- Installing replaces the firmware on the FM-1. What another firmware saved on it may be lost.
- A failed install can leave the FM-1 unable to start. Recovery is felucca-bsp's fm1_rescue (macOS, Linux), which puts the stock firmware back.
- To go back to M-VAVE's firmware: the web installer's "Back to the stock firmware", or M-VAVE's M-UPGRADE.

INSTALL
- In Chrome or Edge: https://jconde007.github.io/nerd-pocket/install/ with the FM-1 connected by USB.
- Or from the command line: fm1-base's tools/fm1_install.py nerdpocket-VERSION.fwsc (needs mido and python-rtmidi).
- If the FM-1 runs a firmware the installer does not recognise, put M-VAVE's firmware back first, then install Nerd Pocket.

CONTROLS
K1-K4: edit the panel. SELECT: next panel. PRESETS: program. ALGORITHM: mod wheel. MASTER: volume.
PROGRAMS: 99, A01-A99. SAVE works on all of them.
HOME, EDIT, ENV, LFO, FX, ARP, GLO, SEQ: open a module; press again for its next panel.
Hold SEL and turn K1-K4: morph amount.
SAVE: keep the sound on this preset, with the pattern and tempo. Hold SEL and press SAVE: back to the factory sound.
SEQ panel: K1 step, K2 note, K3 gate, K4 tempo, SEL step on/off, PLAY start/stop, REC write steps from the keys.
MIDI IN: notes with velocity, mod wheel (CC 1), pitch bend, and Program Change 0-98.
MIDI CLOCK: with MIDI clock coming in (USB or TRS), the arpeggiator (with Sync on), the sequencer and the synced delay follow
  its tempo, and the SEQ panel shows it as Clock. Start restarts the pattern on the next beat, Stop pauses it, Continue resumes it.
  Half a second without clock: back to the synth's own tempo, with the sequencer stopped.
